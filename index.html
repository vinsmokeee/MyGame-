      FAQ

      How do I play?

      Pick a bet under the wheel and tap SPIN. Where the wheel stops decides your prize.

      What can I win?

      Each segment multiplies your bet (×0.5 up to the ×20 Jackpot). The rare Nu 10,000 segment pays Nu 10,000 whatever you bet. LOSE means you lose your bet.

      What is the spin bonus?

      Every 10 spins you get Nu 50 free. The bar under the wheel shows how close you are.

      How do I get more Nu?

      Win on the wheel, collect the spin bonus, or open ☰ Menu → Recharge Nu (minimum Nu 100).

      How do I change my name?

      Tap ✏️ Edit name next to your name at the top.

      How do I contact the owner?

      Tap 💬 Chat or open ☰ Menu → Contact us. You can send messages and photos.

      Is my progress saved?

      Yes, in this browser on this device. Clearing your browser data or switching devices starts a new account.

      Are Nu real money?

      No. Nu in Lucky Spin are play points for fun only. They have no cash value and can't be withdrawn.
